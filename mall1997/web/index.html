THE MALL, 1997

You're sixteen, with $22, a pager and a curfew. About 500 people live their lives in one procedurally generated suburban mall, whether or not you're watching.

This is the Playdate game's own Lua source running in your browser on Lua 5.4, with a stand-in Playdate runtime. It isn't Panic's Simulator: text uses a substitute font at the system font's sizes.

Your first afternoon

  1. On the title screen, A starts a new mall. Generating it takes a moment.
  2. You arrive at the parking lot at 3:25 PM on Friday, August 29. Walk up to the mall doors.
  3. Stand next to someone and press A to talk. Friends page you with plans.
  4. B opens your life menu: pager, bag, people, rumors, the mall directory.
  5. In a record or video store, the crank flips through the racks.
  6. Mom wants you home by 9. The bus leaves from the lot.
