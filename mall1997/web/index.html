THE MALL, 1997

You're sixteen and the new kid in town, with $22, a pager and a curfew. Five kids run the mall. Earn respect on their turf, dig up dirt, call them out, and take it. About 500 people live their lives around you, whether or not you're watching.

This is the Playdate game's own Lua source running in your browser on Lua 5.4, with a stand-in Playdate runtime. It isn't Panic's Simulator: text uses a substitute font at the system font's sizes.

Your first afternoon

  1. On the title screen, A starts a new mall. Generating it takes a moment.
  2. You arrive at the parking lot at 3:25 PM on Friday, August 29. Walk up to the mall doors.
  3. Stand next to someone and press A to talk. Kids on a rival's turf are worth respect; their crew will leak dirt if they like you.
  4. B opens your life menu. The TURF page shows who runs what and how much respect you have.
  5. The kid with the crown runs the place. With enough respect, talk to them and Call them out.
  6. The pawn shop buys anything, including things you didn't pay for.
  7. Mom wants you home by 9. The bus leaves from the lot.
