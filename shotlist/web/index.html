A take in five presses

  1. On the welcome screen, A loads the 60-shot commercial demo.
  2. A logs TAKE +1, then ↑ rates it GREAT.
  3. Hold A circles that take.
  4. → marks GOT IT and jumps to the next priority shot.
  5. Hold B is NEXT from any screen. ↓ is the slate, ↑ the hub.
